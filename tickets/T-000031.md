## Goal
Enforce balanced layout selection rules in the presentation structure generator so the LLM avoids selecting ultra-sparse, image-only layouts that drop substantive pedagogical text and explanations.

## Context & Diagnosis
- In `output/test_slides/google_1794983581.pptx`, Slide 5 and Slide 6 selected image-dominant layouts where the text fields were restricted to single short strings ("Understanding Cellular Nuclei" and "Mitochondria Power"), omitting descriptive bullets explaining ATP synthesis or nuclear transport.
- In `output/test_slides/openrouter_1794984004.pptx`, the `General` template selected layouts with high numbers of micro-card icons (e.g. 13 icon pictures on Slide 3) alongside one hero illustration.
- Presenton's layout selection system prompt (`generate_presentation_structure.py`) currently lacks a rule enforcing that complex concepts must pair a visual hero with at least 2–3 substantive explanation bullets.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Update layout selection guidance in presentation prompt:
  - Require that every scientific concept slide pairs visual imagery with a minimum of 2 explanatory bullets or cards.
  - Discourage selecting title-only or quote-only layouts for dense educational topics.
- [ ] Prefer layouts with balanced aspect ratios (e.g. `title_description_image`, `title_image_bullet_points`, `left_image_right_details_2189`) over single-hero or pure-grid layouts.
- [ ] Filter out repetitive icon card layouts that generate excessive micro-raster images when standard bullet points are more readable.
- [ ] Verify generated 7-slide decks maintain both rich visual diagrams (>50KB) and comprehensive scientific bullet explanations on all slides.

## Implementation Plan
1. Inspect and refine prompt template in `utils/llm_calls/generate_presentation_structure.py`.
2. Add explicit heuristic or validator rejecting slides with fewer than 20 words of explanatory content on educational topics.
3. Re-run benchmark to verify balanced text + diagram distribution across slides.
