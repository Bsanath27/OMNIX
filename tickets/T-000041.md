## Goal
Transform OMNIX slides from text-heavy dashboard cards into rich, visual editorial presentations matching Presenton by:
1. **Uncluttering the Slide Canvas**: Removing internal pedagogical metadata (`BIOLOGY • PEDAGOGICAL TARGETS`, `SLIDE 02 / 04`, `Biology Standard` shield badge) from inside the 16:9 canvas. Slide numbers and grounding badges belong outside the slide frame, allowing headlines and content to breathe with authentic editorial whitespace.
2. **Adding Split-Screen Image & Visual Layouts**:
   - Supporting `hero_visual_split`: 60% structured content on left + 40% high-resolution topic photograph/illustration on right (Presenton's signature slide archetype).
   - Supporting slide-level `image_url` and `image_query` with fallback Unsplash educational image resolver.
3. **LLM Data Chart & Visual Synthesis**:
   - Updating `backend/api/projects.py` slide compilation prompt so the LLM synthesizes:
     - `image_query`: Accurate topic search term for photographic or scientific illustration.
     - `chart_data`: Structured quantitative benchmarks and data points (e.g., rate curves, comparative percentages) for relevant slides.
4. **Interactive Chart & Image Editing in Studio**:
   - Enhancing `SlideStudioView.tsx` with high-resolution image rendering (with image replacement/query prompt capability) and interactive chart rendering.
5. **Compilation & Visual Verification**: Zero TypeScript errors, headless Chrome visual verification across multiple themes.
