## Goal
Eliminate hardcoded 3-card bento canvas in SlideStudioView and replace with Presenton-style dynamic layout engine reflecting selected template theme (Verdant, Nova, Pulse, Signal, etc.).

## Context & Problem
In `frontend/src/components/SlideStudioView.tsx`, the 16:9 canvas is currently hardcoded to:
- A fixed dark `#18181B` container regardless of whether the user chose a light theme (like Verdant or Academic Clean) or a dark theme (like Solar Obsidian).
- A static 3-card bento layout with hardcoded labels (`CARD 01`, `GROUNDED CURRICULUM TOPIC`).
- Titles overflow and clip ("Bioenergetics and ATP S").
- Filmstrip thumbnails are static grey rectangles with mock orange bars.

In Presenton, slides are rendered dynamically based on the chosen design template theme and layout archetype, providing a high-fidelity preview of the actual presentation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] **Theme-Driven Theming:** The 16:9 canvas dynamically applies the active template theme colors (Verdant, Nova, Pulse, Signal, Academic Clean, Solar Obsidian, COL Blue, Standard):
  - Canvas background (light paper or deep dark based on template).
  - Card surfaces, borders, and accent highlights.
  - Heading and paragraph text colors.
- [ ] **Multi-Archetype Slide Rendering:** The canvas dynamically renders different layouts based on slide archetype:
  - `hero_title` / `hero_stat`: Big headline, curriculum level badge, chapter subtitle, and prominent thesis statement.
  - `split_comparison` (2 columns): Balanced side-by-side comparison cards (e.g., Photosynthesis vs Respiration, Animal vs Plant).
  - `card_grid_3` (3 columns): Structured bento concept cards.
  - `bullet_list`: Numbered or icon-bulleted concepts with bold headings and descriptions.
- [ ] **Responsive Title Typography:** Slide titles wrap cleanly without truncation or clipping (fixes SL-01).
- [ ] **Filmstrip Synchronization:** Left-hand thumbnail list reflects the actual slide background, slide numbers, and layout shape.
- [ ] **Teacher Notes & PPTX Parity:** Synchronize speaker notes and ensure slide content matches the compiled PPTX artifact.
