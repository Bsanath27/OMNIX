## Goal
Review and align on architectural design to eliminate inaccurate watermarked stock and blank slide images through a curated NCERT/OpenStax diagram index, domain whitelisting, pre-flight image validation, and in-studio asset swapping.

## Key Discussion Points & Design Decisions

### 1. Curated Textbook Diagram Bank vs. Dynamic Search
- **Option A (Hybrid Recommended):** Maintain a bundled local library of verified, label-accurate scientific diagrams for core curriculum topics (NCERT 9th/10th grade science & OpenStax), falling back to Wikimedia Commons whitelist for arbitrary topics.
- **Option B (Pure Dynamic):** Rely 100% on dynamic web search with aggressive domain whitelisting (Commons/OpenStax) and blacklisting of stock vector sites (`Shutterstock`, `Dreamstime`, `VectorStock`).
- *Decision Needed:* Should we pre-bundle top 50 core science diagrams in `data/assets/diagrams/` for instant, offline, watermark-free rendering?

### 2. Elimination of Blank Images (Puppeteer Race Condition)
- **Root Cause:** Presenton exports slides via headless browser before remote high-res images finish rendering, capturing blank boxes.
- **Proposed Solution:** Implement pre-flight local caching and base64 injection into slide payloads before export begins, guaranteeing zero race conditions.
- *Decision Needed:* Enforce 100% pre-download before slide rendering starts.

### 3. Visual Layout & Typography Balance
- **Issue:** Some layouts generated 13 micro-icons while others had massive image heroes with 1–2 words of text.
- **Proposed Solution:** Standardize on 50/50 and 60/40 split-card archetypes (Hero Diagram on one side, 3 substantive pedagogical takeaway cards with bold lead-ins on the other).
- *Decision Needed:* Approve standardized educational archetype templates.

### 4. In-Studio Asset Swapper UI (Teacher Experience)
- **Proposal:** Provide a 1-click "Change Diagram" floating pill on slide images in OMNIX Studio, offering 4 alternative textbook diagrams and custom search.
- *Decision Needed:* Prioritize integration of `ImageReplacerModal.tsx` in Phase 1 of Slide Studio.
