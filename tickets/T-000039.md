## Problem
When the right Slide Co-Pilot panel (or any right tool drawer) was open, the center 16:9 presentation canvas was crushed horizontally to under 400px width. Because the canvas relied on `aspect-video` without minimum dimensions, its height collapsed to ~200px, causing cards, titles, and text to overflow and become completely unreadable.

## Root Cause
1. `SlideStudioView.tsx`: Canvas had `w-full aspect-video` with no `min-w` or `min-h`, inside a container that lacked `overflow-x-auto`.
2. Left Filmstrip sidebar occupied `w-56` (224px) and right tool drawer occupied `w-84` (336px) + `w-13` dock (52px), consuming over 612px of viewport width.
3. Slide cards had tall textarea rows (`rows={4}` / `rows={3}`), causing vertical overflow.

## Solution
1. **Canvas Minimum Dimensions**: Enforced `min-w-[560px] md:min-w-[640px]` and `min-h-[360px] md:min-h-[400px]` with `aspect-video` and `overflow-x-auto` on the stage, guaranteeing the slide is never crushed.
2. **Compact Filmstrip (`w-44` / 176px)**: Added interactive collapse button in the filmstrip header and a sleek collapsed vertical rail (`w-9`) for 1-click expand/collapse.
3. **Refined Right Drawer (`w-76` / 304px)**: Reduced width and removed synthetic hardcoded mock metrics (`Tokens: 342`, `Latency: 420ms`) from the Co-Pilot panel.
4. **Proportional Typography & Card Sizing**: Dynamic 1-line or 2-line title textarea with `text-xl sm:text-2xl lg:text-3xl`, and tight 3-row card textareas so all content fits with zero clipping.
